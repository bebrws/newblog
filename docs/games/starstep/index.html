A LITTLE GUIDANCE

One step closer.

Help our little explorer reach the star by arranging blocks into a staircase.

  1. Walk up to a block.Use the arrow keys, WASD, or the direction buttons. You can also tap a tile to walk there, or tap a block to approach it.
  2. Choose your next step.A popup offers Climb onto or Push one tile. Climb or descend one height at a time. To push, stand on the ground with an empty floor tile beyond the block. Unavailable actions explain what is in the way.
  3. Make a path to the star.Start with the shortest block. Stairs can turn corners and connect to fixed terraces. Blocks cannot be pulled or stacked.
  4. Room to try again.Press Z to undo and R to restart. Nothing is timed. Rotate the view if something is hidden.

Each choice performs one action. Use the buttons, or press 1 to climb and 2 to push while the popup is open. Press Esc or keep walking to dismiss it. Direction keys follow the view: ↑ moves away into the board; → moves toward its right edge.
